Allowed ingredients

The carnivore rule of thumb: if it came from an animal, it's food. If it grew from the ground, it's not. Salt and water are always fine.

🏷️ Label-reading cheatsheet (for the store)

  • Bacon & ham: ingredients should read roughly “pork, water, salt.” A little sugar in the cure is OK if the label shows 0g sugar per serving — it cooks off.
  • Sausages: the usual trap. Avoid ones listing rusk, breadcrumbs, starch, dextrose, maltodextrin, or soy. Butcher’s plain pork sausage is usually safe.
  • Ground beef: just beef. 80/20 fat ratio keeps you full — very lean mince is a common beginner mistake.
  • Broth: store cartons often have onion, celery, yeast extract. Look for bones + salt only, or make it at home.
  • Canned fish: in water or olive oil is debatable — in water or its own oil is cleanest. Skip anything in sunflower/soybean oil.
  • Cheese: real block cheese only. Pre-shredded has potato starch; “cheese product” slices aren’t cheese.
